## Reproducibility against competitive_intelligence.md

Original method identified: per-season z-scores over every team in the table, sample SD, zero talent kept, defense sign-flipped. Cells matching published figures within rounding: 72 of 72.

Headline: offense PPA, Penn State minus champions (2014-2025) = -1.4 (published -1.40).

Deliberate difference: this module treats zero talent as missing (the original kept those rows). Talent gap against the national table average, 2014-2025 (Penn State z minus comparison z): 1.39 (original method, zero talent kept) vs 1.42 (this module). The difference comes only from treating the 8 zero-talent rows as missing, which also changes the per-season means and SDs used to standardize every team. The published results are preserved unchanged.
